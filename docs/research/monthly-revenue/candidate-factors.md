# Monthly Revenue Candidate Factors

Last updated: 2026-08-07

Candidates are research subjects, not production strategies.

## Tier A — strongest current evidence

### Computer and peripheral equipment + YoY >=20% + MoM >0

Why retained:

- Positive same-industry uplift in the initial industry study.
- Positive behavior in both strong-market and range-market samples.
- D5 and D10 both remained useful in the current sample.
- Sample size is materially larger than many niche industry combinations.

Why not promoted:

- Historical window is still only about 8 revenue months.
- No credible weak-market regime sample yet.
- Needs validation through 2020-2024 history.

## Tier B — continue observing

### YoY >=20% + revenue 3-month high

Initial evidence:

- Strong D5 uplift in several industries.
- Potentially useful as a more immediate revenue-momentum feature.

Need to verify:

- Longer-history stability.
- Whether new-high behavior survives weak markets.
- Industry concentration.

### Consecutive 2 months YoY >=20%

Initial evidence:

- Useful D5/D20 behavior in parts of the sample.
- Potential medium-term persistence signal.

Need to verify longer history and market regimes.

### Consecutive 3 months YoY >=20%

Initial evidence:

- Some strong D20 behavior.
- More consistent with persistent fundamentals than short announcement reaction.

Need to verify whether reduced sample size causes unstable results.

### Biotechnology / medical combinations

Strong initial industry-relative results appeared for high YoY + positive MoM and revenue-new-high definitions.

Keep as an industry research candidate until more market regimes exist.

### Other electronics combinations

Revenue 3-month high and sustained YoY growth showed initial promise.

Keep as observational until history is extended.

## Promotion requirements

No candidate should enter a production strategy until it has:

- Longer history.
- Weak, strong, and range-market observations.
- Sufficient industry-relative sample size.
- Stability across time rather than one or two exceptional months.
- Clear versioned rule definition.
