# TAIWAN STOCK daily video — owner-approved editorial, verification and delivery policy v1

Owner approval: 2026-10-11. Canonical durable rule for the active `daily-gainers-market-opening-end-to-end` project. Read together with `AGENTS.md`, the project's canonical Handoff, its frozen Goal/Phase contracts and source-specific validators. This policy records editorial decisions and the delivery objective; it does NOT claim M5 PASS, grant Prompt B/production/cron/publication authorization, or retroactively alter frozen historical proof. Implement via an appropriately versioned, preregistered A/B round before production integration.

## Immutable brand and presentation
- Exactly: 「大家好，歡迎來到 TAIWAN STOCK！」「追蹤資金，解讀行情。」「錢在哪，我們就在哪！」
- Complete market opening including branding targets 60–90 seconds measured against the actual completed media; 70% professional financial anchor / 30% incisive analyst.
- Spoken script, actual TTS, on-screen display claims and Traditional Chinese SRT are generated from one verified semantic source. Narration and SRT must not omit, contradict or silently paraphrase each other; review timing/word boundaries against final media. Any substantive script edit invalidates affected old TTS/SRT/render proof, and requires regeneration and re-verification of affected material.

## Owner-approved seven-scene editorial intent
1–3. Preserve the three fixed brand lines verbatim.
4. Market movement: first report **rising stock count**, then **falling stock count**, each with 「家」; conclude with the evidence-based market strength/weakness (e.g. 「當天上漲家數四百二十五家，下跌則有五百四十家，市場明顯偏向弱勢。」 is a 2026-10-08 *draft example*, NOT a reusable fact or unconditional conclusion). Avoid reading internal field labels such as 「官方股票欄」. Do not force 「無比價」 or immaterial no-trade classifications into narration. Retain complete original source fields for auditing; never secretly modify counts. A weak day is not proof of a trend reversal.
5. Give verified same-day TWSE **whole-market** trading value and compare with the verified previous-five-trading-day average, with source and scope checked. Do not waste narration on the redundant sentence 「這不是普通股單獨成交額」; still preserve and enforce scope separation inside validation.
6. Give three groups' verified net buy/sell **TWD** and separate individual flows. If compatible same-day turnover and institutional scope is verified, compute the absolute net-buy/sell amount divided by comparable market trading value times 100, correctly label as **淨買賣超相對於成交金額的比率**, not institutions' share of all market transactions or a measured share of gross sell orders. Example 916 ÷ 9245 × 100 ≈ 9.91% is solely a historical DRAFT computation, NOT hardcoded daily values. Net BUY must use buying language, net SELL selling language, zero/mixed data must not be forced into sell-pressure claims; qualify relative pressure from available evidence, not from one ratio alone.
7. Replace repetitive disclaimers with **today's evidence-backed market synthesis and actionable next observations**: combine breadth, turnover relative to recent activity and institutional direction; explain what to monitor next without implying unsupported forecasts or retail counterparties. Data limitations and validation belong in backend gates, not filler narration. Never invent missing evidence.

## Every date and figure is dynamically computed and checked
- On every daily run, resolve the actual intended TWSE trading date and trading calendar; verify original source provenance, timestamps, schema, stock identity/universe, all numbers, units, comparison periods, derived formulas and compatible scopes. No stale-day silent fallback, fixed 2026-10-08 values, hardcoded daily directions, or copy-forward narratives.
- Preserve raw source values and a traceable per-date computation/claims manifest. All narrated and visual numeric claims must trace to validated calculations. If facts, units, scope or interpretation conflict, correct the upstream data/parser/formula or rework unsupported prose **before** synthesis/TTS/media production. Never change data or relax tests to make them green.
- Narration is dynamic based on the day's independently validated figures. Correct arithmetic alone is insufficient if commentary or market direction is misleading.

## Repair-until-delivery operational objective
- A validation or generation failure is a **repair trigger, not a reason to give up that day's video**: diagnose, refresh/re-fetch approved evidence, repair source mapping/formula/narrative or broken workflow, rerun affected validation, and continue through correct script, TTS, captions, render and upload confirmation. Keep bounded checkpointing, meaningful failure logs and rerunnable recovery. Escalate unresolved genuine source/publication blockers clearly; never fabricate data, bypass gates or publish an unverified video simply to satisfy a schedule.
- If an individual optional claim remains unprovable, substitute an evidence-backed, truthful narrative after enforcing the owner-approved required information/coverage contract; do not silently drop mandatory data or claim complete from partial. Non-trading days must be explicitly handled.
- Reuse already verified immutable TTS, captions, MP4, cover and proof where still semantically valid. If only upload fails, retry upload from the preserved artifacts rather than regenerating the video; do not duplicate an already successful publish. Any edited spoken claim requires invalidation/regeneration of the affected dependencies.
- Technical green CI and delivery are distinct. **Delivered** means that the correct day's fully verified video and captions have actually been generated and their intended upload/publication state confirmed. Preserve proof, source versions, media hashes and final links/results. An unresolved error stays visible and does not turn into PASS through retries alone.
- Do not promote production, modify automated schedules, invoke Prompt B or authorize publication merely by recording this policy. Observe the project's original v3 gates and obtain phase-specific authorized implementation and independent verification.

## Market Opening Master fixed-row successor — owner-approved 2026-11-03

For **every trading-date ChatGPT Agent script**, the `market_opening` first scene MUST use the date-verified TWSE market-opening snapshot as one semantic source. The Agent may author thoughtful analysis but MUST NOT choose or reorder the display fields. The single approved Dashboard remains the **current list-row design**, including the opening and closing scene; do not restore historical quadrant/card dashboards. The seven row labels, exact order, and required interpretation are:

1. **加權指數** — official closing level, change points, and change percent (correct direction).
2. **全市場成交金額** — TWSE whole-market TWD value, and ratio to the previous five trading-day average.
3. **上漲／下跌** — TWSE official stock-column breadth with 家.
4. **平盤／漲停** — stock-column unchanged and limit-up counts with 家.
5. **外資／投信／自營商** — separately label each same-day group's buy/sell net TWD amount, not an inferred flow of individual-stock shares.
6. **三大法人合計** — same-day combined institutional buy/sell net amount in TWD.
7. **市場強弱摘要** — a sourced, conditional, concise qualitative conclusion based jointly on breadth, index, turnover and institutions; do not force strength/weakness when directions disagree.

**Agent prompt — mandatory content contract:** Before writing narration, retrieve the date-specific official `market-opening-video/YYYYMMDD.json`; inspect `breadth`, `index`, `trading`, and `institutional` and their dates/scopes. Produce scene one with `scene_type: market_opening`, `stock_codes: []`, `market_opening_claims` and `spoken_values` for all 12 numeric fields (advancers, decliners, unchanged, limit_up, index_close, index_change_points, index_change_pct, turnover_twd, foreign_net_twd, investment_trust_net_twd, dealers_net_twd, total_net_twd). The spoken script MUST explicitly mention ALL these values in natural Mandarin, their direction and units; follow the approved row order when presenting the market opening, where natural grammar permits; also mention the turnover's prior-five-session comparison, and conclude with market-strength/weakness/dispersion reasoning. Preserve the four approved opening lines, 30–90 seconds real audio and exact spoken-to-caption semantic parity. Treat the dashboard as a render of verified data, never as prose supplied by the Agent. Do not invent numerical facts, copy previous trading day, drop any required field, or infer market-wide counterparty activity. Subsequent scenes must cover all date-verified eligible stocks, and closing summary cannot display an arbitrary stock list.

**Fail-closed policy:** Validator must verify each required `spoken_values` phrase really appears in the narration, corresponding `market_opening_claims` equal same-date source fields, five-day average ratio is arithmetically consistent, and fixed row order/value coverage appears in the dashboard; missing or contradictory fields cannot PASS. Missing official input must fail and request source recovery. This successor governs future scripts; older successful MP4s are historical evidence, not retroactively rewritten.

**Embedded zh-TW subtitle policy:** 1080p, centered with symmetric left/right margins, larger readable white text with dark outline, maximum two lines inside the reserved lower subtitle zone beneath the progress bar without overlapping any card or VTuber. Preserve independent SRT; actual FFmpeg burned-in text must be verified with frame evidence. Media and upload gates remain unchanged; main is the single intended integrated implementation, and an isolated test does not substitute for main proof.

Never silently treat the old opening as verified. The Agent-owned stock scenes remain untouched; materialization occurs before the existing video-script validator and before TTS/SRT/render. Legacy verified media remains historical evidence and is not retroactively relabeled. A generated video requires fresh media and caption verification after substantive narration changes.

## Stock-news narration evidence acceptance — owner-approved 2026-11-03

For the Agent-owned stock-list chapters, validate every date-scoped research-news record before TTS. When an actionable source exists, the spoken chapter must identify the actual news publisher or official announcement, its material event and the timing relationship to the trading session. A source link and exact publication timestamp must remain in non-spoken machine-readable evidence attached to the rendered plan. Distinguish verified company news, sector correlation, older background, post-close disclosures, and unverified catalysts; never attribute an intraday price move to a post-close disclosure. News coverage percentage is evidence availability, not a mandate to fabricate an explanation for every 5% gainer. For unsupported stocks, explain differentiated price/volume/institutional/margin/broker-branch facts and what to monitor, rather than repeating a stock-by-stock generic no-catalyst disclaimer.

Before any Render-only TTS, the date-specific validator must fail closed on missing source-to-voice attribution, ungrounded URL/date, omitted key event, post-close causality confusion, duplicate stock coverage and known repetitive filler. Positive and negative regression tests and a dated source-to-voice QA artifact accompany the output. This is a bounded editorial correction within the existing owner-approved Agent stock-research scope, not a replacement for preregistered A/B or permission to publish.


## Owner-approved amendment v1.1 — 2026-11-04: inclusive 5%-gainer coverage and graceful optional-data degradation

**Approval and precedence.** The owner explicitly approves this editorial/operational change for trading dates **20261103 onward**. It supersedes earlier **TWSE-listed common-stock-only** eligibility and **all-or-nothing missing optional field** wording wherever they conflict, including earlier Agent instructions. It does not retroactively change historical A/B proofs, authorize YouTube uploads or remove immutable publication/security gates. The fixed list-row Dashboard and voice/subtitle presentation remain in force.

1. **Eligibility is not security classification.** A date-matched, independently sourced **gain of at least 5%** is the primary candidate criterion. Do not exclude an otherwise reportable security solely for being OTC, ETF, inverse/leveraged ETF, or another exchange-traded product. Label its verified category accurately and explain the different economics where material; never describe an ETF as an operating company. The raw source list may be incomplete: qualify any coverage claim, verify price/change/date for included instruments, and continue reporting sourced entries rather than treating completeness or classification uncertainty alone as a global render blocker. Duplicate identifiers and false, stale or mismatched price data remain errors.

2. **Research may proceed independently.** Search dated company/exchange announcements, credible reporting, product documents and sector context for all discovered gainers **even if** category, market breadth, broker, institutional or other secondary data checks are pending. Respect actual publication timing; later-discovered events may be context, never knowingly portrayed as prior intraday catalysts. If no reliable catalyst is found, distinguish observed price/volume behavior from unproven explanations. The owner does not require a news story for each security.

3. **Optional facts degrade transparently, not fictionally.** Use verified original facts first, then credible external sources with URLs, timestamps, units, security identity and compatible scopes. If an *optional* claim (including individual-instrument institutional flow, margin, broker branch, moving average or catalyst) cannot be sourced, store explicit missing/not-available status and render a neutral dash or 「資料未取得」. Omit unsupported numeric narration rather than replacing null with zero, stale values, inferred values or an invented quote. Preserve any source receipt used. An absent optional datum is **not** by itself cause to cancel the full video or skip a reportable instrument.

4. **Market opening must stay factually coherent.** Keep the seven-row Dashboard labels and row order, but allow a clearly marked unavailable value within a row if its official input is genuinely unavailable after bounded retrieval. Do not announce a missing figure as a verified number, misstate market scope or invent five-day comparisons. Supported fields and their numerical direction/units still require exact source-to-script/display agreement. A partially evidenced opening must never be labeled a fully verified 12-value opening; use an explicit reduced-coverage QA designation until an updated canonical validator and paired A/B acceptance have passed.

5. **Implementation gate.** Update date-scoped generator/schema/validator/render tests to distinguish **core required** (trading date, sourced identity, price/change, truthful claims and media safety) from **optional nullable** fields before granting a production PASS under this amendment. Include a regression case with `00686R` plus a missing optional field, a stale/mismatched-price negative case, source-to-caption consistency and an upload-free Render-only check. Until that code is actually tested, report **POLICY_UPDATED / RENDER_VALIDATOR_PENDING**, not production-ready. Do not bypass a still-active validator to force a green CI or silently weaken unrelated A/B acceptance.

6. **Operational priority.** The existing hourly Agent works forward from **20261103**, continues news research and other independently safe work even while a validator migration is pending, and uses existing candidate → canonical Node validator → promotion, Facts/AI, deterministic opening, script and Render-only pathways whenever their actual contracts pass. Publish locks stay closed; no YouTube upload (including private/unlisted) without a separate explicit owner authorization.
